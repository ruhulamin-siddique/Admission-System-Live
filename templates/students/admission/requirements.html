Engineering & Technology Programs 4-Year B.Sc
Offered Disciplines:
{% for prog in eng_programs %} {{ prog.short_name|default:prog.name }} {% empty %} CSE EEE ME IPE CE ICT {% endfor %}
Minimum Academic Requirements:
  • HSC / Alim (Science): Must have passed HSC or equivalent examination from Science group with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry. Minimum GPA 3.00 in each of SSC & HSC.
  • Combined GPA: Total GPA of SSC and HSC must be at least 7.00 (including 4th subject).
  • Subject Requirement: Minimum Grade Point (GP) of 3.00 individually in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry in HSC examination.
  • English Medium (GCE): Minimum 5 subjects in O-Level and 2 subjects (Physics, Chemistry & Math) in A-Level with minimum GPA 2.50 in each.
  • Diploma Candidates: Diploma in Engineering holders from Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB) with minimum CGPA 3.00 out of 4.00 are eligible to apply.
  • Direct Admission: Candidates having combined GPA 9.00 or above in SSC & HSC may be eligible for Direct Admission without Admission Test (subject to seat availability).
Business, Arts & General Programs BBA, BA, MBA, MA
Offered Disciplines:
{% for prog in non_eng_programs %} {{ prog.short_name|default:prog.name }} {% empty %} BBA BBA in AIS BA (Hons) in English MBA MA in English {% endfor %}
Minimum Academic Requirements:
  • BBA & BBA in AIS: Candidates from Science, Business Studies, or Humanities group. Minimum GPA 3.00 in each of SSC and HSC, with a combined total GPA of at least 6.00.
  • BA (Hons) in English: Candidates from any discipline with minimum GPA 3.00 in each of SSC & HSC (combined GPA >= 6.00) and minimum Grade Point 3.00 in English.
  • Direct Admission: Combined GPA 8.00 or above in SSC and HSC allows direct admission without written test.
  • Graduate Programs (MBA / MA): Bachelor's degree from any recognized university with minimum CGPA 2.50 or 2nd division in graduation.
Mandatory Documents & Application Checklist Required for Admission

Applicants are required to keep the following documents and information ready before filling out the online application form:

  • 1. Digital Passport Size Photograph: Recent passport-sized color photo (300 x 300 pixels, formal attire, clean white background, maximum size 300 KB in JPG/PNG format).
  • 2. SSC / Equivalent Details: Roll number, registration number, board name, passing year, and achieved GPA.
  • 3. HSC / Equivalent Details: Roll number, registration number, board name, passing year, and achieved GPA.
  • 4. Active Mobile Number: A valid personal mobile number. (Crucial: This mobile number will be used for two-factor admit card download and all official SMS updates).
  • 5. bKash Transaction ID (TRXID): Complete payment of the application form fee via bKash "Send Money" to the university bKash merchant/agent number ({{ active_intake.bkash_number|default:"01769675556" }}) and copy the Transaction ID.
  • 6. National ID / Birth Certificate: NID or Birth Registration Certificate number.
  • 7. Guardian Information: Father's and Mother's full name, occupation, and contact mobile numbers.
  • 8. Addresses: Detailed Present and Permanent address (House, Village/Street, Post Code, Police Station, and District).
